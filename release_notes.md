# Sleeve v2.0: The Expressive Update 🎨

Welcome to the biggest update to Sleeve yet! This major release brings a complete visual overhaul, powerful new customization options, a vastly improved search engine, and a brand-new app icon.

### ✨ What's New
* **Expressive Material Redesign**: A massive visual overhaul honoring modern design principles with softer curves, improved typography, and buttery-smooth page transitions.
* **Dynamic Island Dock**: Completely customizable bottom navigation bar! You can now choose exactly which tabs (Home, Albums, Artists, Genres, Composers, etc.) appear in your main dock.
* **All-New Smart Search Engine**: Instant contextual search across your entire library with dedicated category filtering for Tracks, Albums, Artists, Composers, and Genres.
* **Comprehensive Settings Hub**: We've unified all app preferences into a clean, easy-to-use Settings menu.
* **Filter Short Tracks**: Keep your music library clean! A new setting allows you to automatically hide audio files (like WhatsApp voice notes or ringtones) shorter than 60 seconds.
* **Keep Screen Awake**: A new toggle to prevent your screen from timing out while you're browsing music or viewing the player.
* **New High-Res App Icon**: A brand-new sleek, vector-based app icon that scales flawlessly and crystal-clear on your mobile home screen.

### 🛠 Fixes & Improvements
* **Audio Focus Reliability**: Fixed frustrating audio focus bugs—music now properly restores volume after notifications and handles external interruptions seamlessly.
* **Performance Enhancements**: Deep optimizations to the caching engine, isolate-based background library scanning, and alphabetical indexing for blazing-fast performance on massive libraries.
* Removed the clunky audio routing popups in favor of native OS routing for much higher stability.
* Increased the scrolling gutter space at the bottom of lists so content is never hidden behind the navigation dock.

Enjoy the music! 🎧
